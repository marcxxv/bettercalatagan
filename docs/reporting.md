# Corrections and sensitive reports

Ordinary factual corrections belong in GitHub issues, with a source and page URL.
Issues are public. Never attach identity documents, personal contact details, payment
records, confidential legal notices or vulnerability exploit details.

## Private channel: setup required

The maintainer confirmed on 11 October 2026 that no reporting email is available.
No placeholder, personal commit email or resort inbox is published as a substitute.
Before inviting sensitive reports, create a dedicated inbox, verify receiving and
replying, assign an accountable maintainer, and add the working contact to
`/about#contribute`, CONTRIBUTING, LICENSES and this document. Assess controller
identification, rights notices and provider arrangements under the Data Privacy Act.
This remains a publication-readiness condition, not a functioning private channel.

## Handling a report once the channel exists

1. Acknowledge receipt when the maintainer reviews the inbox; do not promise a fixed
   response time. Ask for the affected page and minimum context needed to investigate.
2. Assess urgency: exposed credentials, sensitive personal data, mistaken identity
   or an immediate safety risk warrant priority. Restrict affected material temporarily
   where a credible harm justifies it, without presuming an accusation is true.
3. Preserve only necessary evidence privately with access restricted to the reviewer.
   Do not copy private attachments into GitHub, the public repository or AI prompts.
4. Check original records, date, lawful purpose and the reporter's requested remedy.
   Request identity evidence only when necessary and proportionate. Consult Philippine
   counsel for disputed legal notices or unclear rights and statutory deadlines.
5. Correct, contextualise or remove material when justified. Update the page, exports,
   search and assistant corpus together; arrange cache invalidation and confirm Worker
   re-indexing where needed. Rotate exposed credentials, rather than just deleting them.
6. Reply privately with the decision and reasons. Record a minimal public changelog
   entry that does not reveal the reporter or private evidence. Delete private evidence
   when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

No arbitrary takedown demand or unsupported allegation is accepted automatically.
Public-interest records remain available unless a justified correction or restriction
is needed. This process must not delay a legally required response or remedy.
