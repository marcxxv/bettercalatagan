## D. Legal and regulatory review

**Municipal illustration: unresolved, preserved.** RA 7160 §22(a)(3), (b) addresses LGU corporate
seals, continued use, modification and registration. It does not itself grant this private
project permission. RA 8491 §§44–46 regulates governmental heraldic adoption/registration;
those provisions do not establish a blanket private historical-use exception. Searches did
not yield an applicable Calatagan ordinance or permission; the former municipal site is offline.
Absence of a search result is not proof that no rule exists. Ask Philippine counsel to check
local ordinances, authorised reproduction, the supplied reference's rights and context before
making a definitive legality claim. No binding prohibition applicable to this exact rendition
was established in this audit. [RA 7160](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/53542),
[RA 8491](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4626).

**Copyright: distinguish authorship and other rights.** RA 8293 §175 excludes specified subject
matter including mere data; §176.1 addresses government works and prior approval for exploitation
for profit; §176.3 preserves third-party rights in government publications. Public access does
not grant every image, mark, attachment or platform asset a reusable licence. MIT covers code;
CC BY covers the independent prose/compilation, not source documents or official symbols.
The reader passes source bytes through infrastructure caching; it is not a maintained mirror.
Portfolio visibility is not analysed here as automatically exempt from any applicable profit-use
rule. Have counsel assess this specific use if disputed. [RA 8293](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4371).

**Defamation and public-interest expression: contextual protections, not immunity.** RA 10175
§4(c)(4) and the Supreme Court's Disini decision address online libel. Borjal discusses qualified
public-interest commentary and actual malice in its factual setting. Neither makes all
publicly sourced statements safe. Contract figures, bidder counts and missing data are not proof
of criminal conduct. Keep neutral, dated attribution, document corrections, and seek counsel
for an actual allegation or legal notice. Article III §§4 and 7 protect expression and access to information subject to applicable
limits; these do not remove other legal obligations. [1987 Constitution](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/3/353). [Disini v. Secretary of Justice](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/56650),
[Borjal v. Court of Appeals](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/29796).

**Privacy: evaluate each processing activity.** RA 10173 §4 has specific exclusions for
role-related government/contract information and specified purposes. Aggregate statistics
are generally distinct from information identifying a natural person; archived attachments,
sole traders' contacts, request identifiers and free-text questions require a separate scope
assessment. §§11–14, 16, 20–21 address proportionality/lawful basis, sensitive information,
processors, notices/rights, security and accountability. A no-analytics design does not settle
these obligations. Assess controller identification/contact, rights handling, provider terms,
retention and transfers; no blanket journalistic/research exemption is assumed for all assistant
operations. [RA 10173, NPC text](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/).

NPC Advisory 2026-01 explains that publicly available personal information is not blanket
consent and addresses lawful basis, minimisation and privacy-impact assessment for scraping.
NPC Advisory 2024-04 addresses personal-data processing with AI systems. Apply them to actual
personal information, not as a reason to remove sourced aggregate civic facts. A practical
privacy impact assessment and provider review are appropriate before broadly promoting the AI
feature. [NPC 2026-01](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SGD_A_1.pdf),
[NPC 2024-04](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Advisory-2024.12.19-Guidelines-on-Artificial-Intelligence-w-SGD.pdf).

NPC Circular 2022-04 registration scope includes specified size/data/risk triggers; a small
project is not automatically exempt solely because it has few maintainers. Actual registration
applicability was not established from account/data-volume evidence. Review scope with a privacy
professional. [NPC registration reminder](https://privacy.gov.ph/reminder-on-mandatory-data-protection-officer-and-data-processing-system-registration/).

Statutes/decisions were fetched read-only with TLS verification and supplied intermediate
certificates where needed. The browsing service failed on some E-Library pages; the direct
certificate-verified downloads succeeded. This records what was consulted, not a guarantee
that no later amendment, local ordinance or fact-dependent rule applies. Political or
reputational pressure remains distinct from a proven legal breach.

## H. Production readiness and exact release steps

The changes are **review-ready, conditional for production**. No schema migration is required.
Keep production unchanged until the maintainer approves release.

1. Review this branch/diff, legal conditions and `docs/reporting.md`. Configure/test the private
   inbox and add its exact address to the documented locations before inviting sensitive reports.
2. Enable main protection requiring PR approval and the existing CI job
   `Typecheck, lint, civic-data tests, build`. Review asset/local-seal/provider conditions below.
3. Once authorised, push `codex/prepublication-hardening`, open a PR and check remote CI/Vercel
   preview. Re-run `npm ci`, `npm run check`, `npm run validate:data`; in Worker run `npm ci`
   and `npm run typecheck`. Preview header/reader checks are necessary because Astro preview
   does not implement Vercel header policy.
4. Test the reviewed Worker with live configured models on **separate** D1/Vectorize resources
   and a test config. Do not run ordinary `wrangler dev` against the production remote index.
   Use `node worker/eval/run.mjs --url <test-worker-url> --origin <allowed-preview-origin>`;
   review actual answers for period attribution, numeric units and allegations as well as pass
   patterns. Include the appended hardening category. Resolve failures before promotion.
5. After explicit production approval, deploy the reviewed Worker from `worker/` with
   `npm run deploy`, then merge the reviewed site PR to main (which triggers Vercel). Keep the
   previous Worker version available for rollback. Confirm the Vercel deployment is READY,
   live independence/privacy/directory copy, JSON exports, reader and header behaviour.
6. Confirm corpus refresh completes across bounded scheduled batches (over 1,187 passages may span
   several runs). Evaluate the approved production Worker using its exact allowed origin;
   verify /health and public source links. Do not publish LinkedIn claims about this release
   until those checks pass.

Rollback: revert the site review commit through a reviewed PR and restore the prior Worker
version. If AI answers are unreliable, disable the public assistant endpoint and redeploy the
site; deterministic search and all civic pages remain useful. Corrections/removals must also
update exports, search/corpus and relevant edge caches.
